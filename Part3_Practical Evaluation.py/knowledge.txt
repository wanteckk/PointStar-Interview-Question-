PointStar Customer Support Knowledge Base

Refund Policy:
Customers can request a refund within 14 days of purchase.
Refunds are only available for eligible purchases.
Refund requests submitted after 14 days are normally not eligible.

Technical Support:
For technical problems, customers should provide their account ID,
a description of the problem, and relevant error messages.

Security:
If a customer reports a security breach or suspected account compromise,
the issue should be escalated to a human support agent immediately.
